How it works

How a liquid oral manufacturing plant works

A liquid oral (syrup) manufacturing plant prepares, mixes, filters and stores oral liquids such as syrups, suspensions and solutions in a closed, hygienic system, ready for bottle filling.

Where it is used

Cough & cold syrupsOral suspensionsTonics & oral solutionsOther liquid medicines

  1. Material handling

    Sugar is transferred into the process by a vacuum system instead of manual loading.

  2. Sugar syrup preparation

    Sugar is dissolved in a jacketed, insulated vessel with a bottom-entry propeller mixer for vigorous mixing.

  3. Pre-filtration

    The sugar syrup passes through a back-washable stainless-steel sintered filter.

  4. Solution preparation

    Actives and excipients are mixed in process vessels; an in-line high-shear mixer is used for suspensions.

  5. Storage & transfer

    The finished solution is held in storage vessels and moved by lobe pumps through sanitary piping.

  6. Automation & CIP

    A PLC system runs the process and an automated CIP system cleans vessels and lines between batches.
