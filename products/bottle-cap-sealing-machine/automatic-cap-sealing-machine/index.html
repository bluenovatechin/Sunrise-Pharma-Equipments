How it works

An automatic capping machine places and tightens caps on filled bottles at a set torque, so every bottle is sealed the same way at production speed.

  1. Cap feeding

    An automatic cap elevating system feeds caps to the capping head.

  2. Bottle in

    Filled bottles arrive on the conveyor and are positioned under the head.

  3. Tighten to torque

    The capping mechanism disengages automatically once the preset torque is reached, so caps are not damaged.

  4. Bottle out

    Sealed bottles continue down the line to labelling.
