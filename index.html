Listed prices range from ₹3,50,000 to ₹31,50,000 per unit, depending on the machine. Prices are indicative — the final quote depends on capacity, material and automation, so contact us for a quotation.

Yes. Customisation is one of our core strengths — we provide customised, end-to-end solutions, fully automated with PLC controls or semi-automated as required.

Yes. We have a highly experienced team for installation and validation support.
